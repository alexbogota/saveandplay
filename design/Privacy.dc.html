Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information SaveAndPlay ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website, and how it is used.

Information we collect

Usage data. We use web analytics tools to understand how the site is used: pages visited, links clicked, device type, approximate location and referring site. This data is collected in aggregate.

Messages you send. If you contact us, we receive your name, email address and message, and use them only to reply.

Cookies and affiliate tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for basic site functions and analytics.

Some links on the site are affiliate links. When you click one, the store or affiliate network that runs the program may set a cookie, so that a purchase you make can be credited to us. These parties have their own privacy policies. See our affiliate disclosure for details.

How we use information

We use the information above to run and improve the site, understand which deals are useful, answer your messages and measure affiliate performance. We don't sell your personal information.

Third-party sites

SaveAndPlay links to game stores and other websites. We don't control them and aren't responsible for their privacy practices. When you buy from a store, your data is handled under that store's own policy.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask what information we hold about you, or to have it deleted. To make a request, email contact@saveandplay.com.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
