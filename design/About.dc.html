ABOUT

Save on the games. Play more of them.

SaveAndPlay is an independent site that highlights discounts on PC games. We collect offers from established game stores in one place, so you can see what is cheap and go straight to the store.

What we do

We list current deals, bundles and free games from stores such as Fanatical, Green Man Gaming, GOG, Humble Store and Gamesplanet. Each deal shows the store, the discount and the price, with a link to the store's own page.

We don't sell games. You always pay the store directly, and your game goes into your own account there.

How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through one, the store may pay us a small commission. It doesn't change the price you pay.

Read the full affiliate disclosure.

Why SaveAndPlay exists

Game prices change all the time, and the best offer is rarely in the first store you check. SaveAndPlay is an independent project built to make that search quicker: one place to see good PC game deals, bundles and freebies, without opening store after store.

We link only to established stores, and we show prices as clearly as we can. We can't guarantee that every price is current, so please check the final price in the store before you buy.
